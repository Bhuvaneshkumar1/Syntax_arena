🕹️ How to Obtain 3D Arcade Trophies

All 3D Cartridge Trophies are permanent cryptographic achievements minted to your player identity. There are two primary pathways to acquire them:

⚔️ 1. In-Game Feats & Accomplishments

Solve arena questions, maintain winning streaks (3🔥, 5🔥, 8🔥), graduate language tracks in the Cyber Academy, conquer 1v1 duels, and achieve 100% precision on Expert tiers to automatically forge cartridges into your vault.

🪙 2. Arcade Points Redemption

Earn Points by finishing arena sessions and memory lab puzzles. Unlocked badges can be acquired directly in the Battlestation / Settings shop using your spendable career points.

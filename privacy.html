01

Overview & Application Scope

SyntaxArena ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is a browser-native, gamified computer science learning environment and tactile memory laboratory available at https://syntaxarena-phi.vercel.app.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of SyntaxArena, including those who play anonymously in local Guest Mode, those who create email-based accounts, and those who authenticate through third-party OAuth providers such as Google Sign-In or GitHub OAuth.

Core Privacy Principle: SyntaxArena was built with an offline-first, client-side architectural foundation. We do not sell user data, run third-party tracking scripts, inject commercial advertising trackers, or monitor your activity across external websites.
02

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

When you choose to sign in to SyntaxArena using your Google Account, we request access only to your basic public profile information and email address to verify your identity and save your gameplay progress.

SyntaxArena's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, regarding data accessed through Google OAuth 2.0:

  • No Restricted Scopes: We do NOT request access to Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or any sensitive Google workspace files. We only request basic identity scopes: openid, profile, and email.
  • No Advertising Usage: Google user data is NEVER used or transferred to third-party serving advertising, retargeting, or interest-based profiling.
  • No Human Inspection: Human employees, contractors, or third parties do not inspect or read your private Google data.
  • Limited Use Adherence: Data obtained via Google Sign-In is exclusively used to provide and enhance user-facing features (player identity, game level synchronizing, and profile avatars) within the SyntaxArena game.
03

Depending on how you interact with SyntaxArena, we may collect the following categories of data:

04

How We Use Your Information

We process collected information strictly for the following operational and pedagogical purposes:

  • Authentication & Session Management: Authenticating your identity so you can securely log in, sign out, or restore your session across browser tabs.
  • Cross-Device Cloud Synchronization: Enabling you to access your solved code challenges, memory lab completions, and battlestation visual unlocks on any device.
  • Profile & Visual Customization: Displaying your selected callsign, rank badge, and avatar on the Topbar, Sidebar, and Trophy Vault.
  • Leaderboard & Fair Play Verification: Calculating XP rankings and preventing automated bot scripts or game integrity violations.
  • Customer Support: Responding to user inquiries, account recovery questions, or data export/deletion requests.
05

Storage, Security & Zero-Sale Guarantee

Zero-Sale Guarantee: SyntaxArena will never sell, lease, monetize, or trade your personal information, email address, or gaming telemetry to any data brokers, marketers, or third-party advertisers. Period.

Security Measures: We implement rigorous technical and organizational controls to protect your data:

  • End-to-End Encryption in Transit: All web traffic, API calls, and OAuth handshakes strictly enforce modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Isolated Storage Architecture: Authentication metadata is isolated in browser storage under syntaxarena_auth_v1, segregated from game telemetry to avoid cross-contamination.
  • Minimal Server Footprint: Authentication token exchanges are executed via transient serverless functions hosted securely on Vercel without persistent database logging of OAuth secret credentials.
06

Data Retention & User Deletion Rights (Right to be Forgotten)

We believe you have absolute ownership over your data. You may inspect, export, or permanently erase your data at any time:

  • Self-Service Account Deletion: You can wipe your account and reset all local storage at any time by visiting Settings > Data Management and selecting "Reset / Delete Profile".
  • Revoking Google Permissions: You can disconnect SyntaxArena from your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Security Dashboard at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Email Data Deletion Requests: You may email our developer support team at support@syntaxarena.cloud with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All associated cloud sync records will be permanently purged within 48 hours.
07

Cookies & Local Storage Technology

SyntaxArena does not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or commercial marketing pixels (e.g. Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Ads remarketing).

We utilize standard, first-party HTML5 Web Storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) exclusively for essential application functionality:

  • Saving your active color theme preference (dark/light) and audio volume settings.
  • Preserving in-game question progress and unlocked achievements while offline.
  • Maintaining your authenticated session state across page reloads.
08

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SyntaxArena is designed as an educational programming tool. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Students under 13 are encouraged to use SyntaxArena in anonymous Guest Mode, which functions 100% offline without collecting emails, full names, or external identity tokens. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to our cloud sync, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.
